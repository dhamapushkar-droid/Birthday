Simple tips for decorating a birthday at home

  • Pick one or two main colours. A tight colour scheme looks neat and planned, even with simple balloons.
  • Decide the photo spot first. Put your best balloons and the name décor behind the cake table, because that is where most photos are taken.
  • Inflate balloons the same day. They stay firmer and look fresher on the day of the party.
  • Keep the poppers for one big moment. Use them when the cake is cut so everyone is ready with their phones.
  • Order a few days early. This leaves time for custom name items to be made and for you to fix anything last minute.
