A birthday looks very different when the décor carries the birthday child's own name. Guests notice it, the photos look planned, and the child feels that the party was made for them.

Custom name items are what make PartyNest different. Tell us the name, the age and the colours or theme you like, and we will make personalised party décor for your celebration in Delhi NCR.

How a custom order works

  1. Contact us. Use our Contact section.
  2. Share the details. Name, age, colours or theme, party date and delivery area.
  3. We confirm. We tell you what can be made and whether it can reach you by your party date.
  4. We deliver. Your custom items arrive at your address in Delhi NCR.

What to tell us

  • The name, spelled exactly. Send it in writing so there is no mistake.
  • The age. Useful if the décor should show the age of the birthday child.
  • Colours or theme. Even a simple idea such as pink and gold helps.
  • The party date. This tells us how much time there is to make your items.
  • Your delivery area. Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad or Faridabad.

Tips for a smooth custom order

  • Order as early as you can, because custom items are made for you.
  • Double-check the spelling of the name before you confirm.
  • Pair your name décor with balloons in the same colours for a matching look. See birthday decor.
  • Keep the name décor behind the cake table, where most photos are taken.
