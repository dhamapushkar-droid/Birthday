Quick tips by occasion

  • Birthday: choose one or two main colours and put the name décor behind the cake table.
  • Baby shower or welcome baby: soft, light colours look calm and work well in photos.
  • Anniversary: keep it simple with a small balloon cluster and one personal touch.
  • Naming ceremony: light, traditional colours suit a family function better than loud ones.
  • Graduation or farewell: use the colours of the school, college or team to tie the décor together.
