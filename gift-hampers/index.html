How to choose a good hamper

  • Think about the person first. Their age and what they enjoy matter more than a big price tag.
  • Set a budget before you call. It helps us suggest something that fits instead of guessing.
  • Add a personal touch. A name or a short message makes any gift feel chosen for them.
  • Check the delivery date. If the gift must arrive on the birthday itself, tell us the date when you order.
  • Pair it with the party. Sending a hamper along with balloons and a name banner makes the surprise complete. See our birthday decor.

How to order a gift hamper
